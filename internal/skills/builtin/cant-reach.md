---
name: cant-reach
description: An app works at home but not away (or the other way round), or a link says the site can't be reached.
audience: friends
services: [tunnel]
---
1. Call status. Look for "Outside access" (the Cloudflare tunnel) or anything marked "because ... is down".
2. Tunnel down: away from home nothing will load until Wizard fixes it. At home, the local address may still work.
3. Everything up: ask whether they're on mobile data or Wi-Fi, and which link they use. Old bookmarks with an IP address only work at home.
4. Use tell_owner with the app and the link they used if it still fails.
