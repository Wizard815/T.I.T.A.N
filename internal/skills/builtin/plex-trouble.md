---
name: plex-trouble
description: Plex won't load, buffers, says unavailable, or works at home but not away.
audience: friends
services: [plex, tunnel]
---
1. Call status. Look at Plex and anything it depends on (the tunnel, "Outside access").
2. Plex down because Outside access is down: say that. Away from home won't work until Wizard fixes the tunnel; at home on Wi-Fi it may still work.
3. Plex up but buffering: look at Plex's info line. Many people watching, or videos "converting", load the server. Suggest picking a lower quality, or "Original" if their device supports it.
4. One film or episode won't play while others do: the file may be broken. Use tell_owner with the title. Wizard has a tool that finds broken files.
5. Never restart Plex while people are watching (the info line says how many), unless the owner asks.
